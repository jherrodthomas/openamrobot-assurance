## 1. Purpose

How the project identifies, analyses, treats and monitors project risks: events that could stop the fork from achieving the assurance path on its planned scope, effort and quality. Product risks belong to the risk assessment (WP-C-03), not here. Strategic decisions D-nn of WP-M-01 §8 are logged in §5.

## 2. Risk management process

Identification sources (gap assessment, WP-M-01 §9, gate reviews, QA and confirmation findings, upstream changes); probability and impact scales 1–5; exposure = P × I with High ≥ 15, Medium 8–14, Low ≤ 7; treatments avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept; monthly review and escalation.

## 3. Roles

Project maintainer owns the register; safety manager agrees accepted risks that touch a safety claim.

## 4. Risk register

R-nn rows: description, P, I, exposure, treatment, owner, trigger, status. Seeded from WP-M-01 §9: no funding for safety hardware; one person in every role; 2.0 overtaking Gate A; no licensed standards; the AI Act threshold; upstream Wi-Fi and ROS 2 domain exposure during tests.

## 5. Decision log

D-01…D-14 of WP-M-01 §8 with the register entry, the answer and where it was applied. The decision register is the place a question is asked; this log is where a strategic decision is recorded once applied.
