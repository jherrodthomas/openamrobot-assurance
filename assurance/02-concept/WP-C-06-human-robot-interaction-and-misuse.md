## 1. Purpose

Analyses how people interact with the robot in each task and zone, what collaborative operation (ISO 10218-2, ISO/TS 15066) would require when the arms come, and the reasonably foreseeable misuse of WP-C-01, with the approach speeds and distances of ISO 13855 as the quantitative basis for personnel detection.

## 2. Interaction scenarios

Zones and people presence are taken from WP-C-02; per scenario: who, where, how close, how fast, what they are doing.

## 3. Approach speeds and safety distances

ISO 13855 walking speed 1.6 m/s, the robot's speed and stopping distance (WP-S-04), the resulting minimum detection distance per zone.

## 4. Collaborative operation (arms, later)

Power and force limits of ISO/TS 15066 as interface assumptions for the arms; not claimed for the base.

## 5. Reasonably foreseeable misuse

Each misuse of WP-C-01 §5 rated for likelihood and consequence, with the measure that addresses it (design, safeguard, information).

## 6. Operator interface and modes

Teleoperation, autonomous, docking, maintenance; how mode is selected and shown; the pendant E-stop.
