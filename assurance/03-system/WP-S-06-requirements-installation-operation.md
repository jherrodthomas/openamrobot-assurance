## 1. Purpose

Requirements on how the robot is installed, operated, maintained and decommissioned so that the safety functions keep working: commissioning checks, periodic tests, replacement intervals, secure configuration, battery handling, disposal.

## 2. Installation and commissioning

Commissioning test of each safety function; zone setup; parameter loading and its verification.

## 3. Operation

Pre-use checks, modes, what the operator may and may not do; secure operation guidelines (62443-4-1 SG).

## 4. Maintenance

Proof tests and intervals for the chain (13849-1 §11), battery, brakes, scanner window; firmware updates under WP-P-10.

## 5. Decommissioning

Battery, data, keys.
