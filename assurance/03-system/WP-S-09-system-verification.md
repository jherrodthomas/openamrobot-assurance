## 1. Purpose

Verification that each safety function achieves its required PL (ISO 13849-1 §8) and behaves as specified under the faults of ISO 13849-2: the comparison of the achieved PL from WP-H-04 with the PLr, the systematic measures, and the fault behaviour tests.

## 2. PL verification

Per safety function: PLr, category, MTTFD, DCavg, CCF score, achieved PL, margin; systematic failure measures applied.

## 3. Fault behaviour

Results of WP-V-05 against the fault lists; fault exclusions and their justification.

## 4. Electrical verification

EN 60204-1 §18 checks: continuity of the protective bonding circuit, insulation, functional tests.

## 5. Conclusion

Per safety function: verified or not, with the open items.
