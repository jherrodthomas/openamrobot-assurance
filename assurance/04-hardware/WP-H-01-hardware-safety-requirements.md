## 1. Purpose

Hardware safety requirements for the safety chain, the braking system and the detection hardware: HWSR-nn derived from WP-S-02 and WP-S-03, with the category, DC and environmental requirements each component must meet.

## 2. Requirements

HWSR-nn: relay type and inputs, contactor ratings and mirror contacts, brake type (engaged on power loss), scanner type and fields, E-stop devices, fuse and disconnect, wiring and connectors, enclosure.

## 3. Environmental and electrical requirements

Temperature, vibration, IP rating, supply tolerance, EMC.

## 4. Traceability

To WP-S-02 and WP-S-03; verified by WP-H-06.
