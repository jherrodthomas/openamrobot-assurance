## 1. Purpose

Failure analysis of the safety chain hardware: an FMEA per block against the fault lists of ISO 13849-2 Annexes A–D, the fault exclusions claimed and their justification, and the diagnostic coverage each detected fault contributes.

## 2. Method

IEC 60812 FMEA at block and component level; ISO 13849-2 fault lists per technology (electrical, electronic, mechanical, pneumatic not used).

## 3. FMEA tables

Per block: failure mode, effect on the safety function, detection, DC contribution, fault exclusion claimed.

## 4. Fault exclusions

Each with the ISO 13849-2 basis and the design measure that justifies it.
