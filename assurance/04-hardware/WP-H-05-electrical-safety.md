## 1. Purpose

Electrical safety of the robot under EN 60204-1: supply disconnecting device, overcurrent protection, protection against electric shock, protective bonding, the battery and charger, and the verification tests of §18. Closes GAP-19, GAP-20, GAP-21, GAP-24 in design.

## 2. Supply and disconnection

Battery disconnect, fuse sizing against the pack's short-circuit current, charger input protection.

## 3. Protection against electric shock

230 V side: enclosure, RCD, earthing; 24 V side: SELV/PELV classification.

## 4. Battery system

Lead-acid now, LiFePO4 with BMS in 2.0; charging, source selection, regenerative braking path (GAP-24).

## 5. Verification

EN 60204-1 §18 tests and their records.
