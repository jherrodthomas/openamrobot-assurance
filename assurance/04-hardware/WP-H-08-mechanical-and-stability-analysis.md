## 1. Purpose

Mechanical and stability analysis of the robot: mass and centre of gravity (measured), payload, tip-over margin under braking and on slopes, ISO 3691-4 stability requirements, pinch and crush points, guarding. Closes GAP-23.

## 2. Mass properties

Measured mass, centre of gravity with and without payload, track and wheelbase as built (0.46 m track, GAP-23).

## 3. Stability

Static and dynamic tip-over margins; slope limit; the maximum deceleration the robot can sustain without tipping. The maximum deceleration is an input to the stopping budget of WP-S-04.

## 4. Pinch, crush and shear points

Wheels, battery tray, future arm envelope; guarding or distance per ISO 13854/13857.

## 5. Structural

Battery fixing (Rev B pack "placed only"), fasteners, enclosure.
