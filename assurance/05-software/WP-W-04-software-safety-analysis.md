## 1. Purpose

Software safety analysis of the firmware and the ROS 2 chain: a software FMEA (IEC 60812) of the components of WP-W-03, failure modes SWF-nn, their effect on demand on the chain, and the measures.

## 2. Method and scope

Per component of WP-W-03; failure modes of input, timing, state, resource and interface.

## 3. Analysis tables

SWF-nn → effect → detection → measure → requirement.

## 4. Findings

Seeded from GAP-06, GAP-07, GAP-09, GAP-37, GAP-38.
