## 1. Purpose

ML engineering for any learned component: data management, training, evaluation, deployment and monitoring, so that the classification of WP-C-09 holds and the evidence the AI Act would require exists if the threshold is crossed.

## 2. Data

Provenance, coverage of the operating environment, labelling, versioning.

## 3. Training and evaluation

Pinned models, evaluation against WP-C-10 triggering conditions, perturbation tests.

## 4. Deployment and monitoring

Version pinning, runtime monitors, rollback.
