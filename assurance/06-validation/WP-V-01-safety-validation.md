## 1. Purpose

The safety validation plan, specification and report (ISO 13849-1 §10, ISO 13849-2): the validation of every safety function on the robot against WP-S-02, by analysis and test, by persons independent of the design where practicable, with the individual campaigns WP-V-02…V-05 as its parts.

## 2. Validation plan

Principles, independence, environment, the robot configuration and baseline, the campaigns and their order.

## 3. Validation specification

VAL-nn per safety function: analysis items and tests; acceptance criteria.

## 4. Report

Results, deviations, residual open items, recommendation to the gate.
