## 1. Purpose

Measurement of stopping distance, braking and stability of the robot: per speed, load, floor and stop type (E-stop, protective stop, watchdog coast); tip-over tests on the slope limit. The measured stopping distance feeds the field dimensions validated in WP-V-03.

## 2. Test specification

ST-nn: speeds, loads, floors, stop types, repetitions, measurement method (marked floor, video, encoder log).

## 3. Stability tests

Braking on the slope limit, cornering, payload at the limit; ISO 3691-4 stability tests as applicable.

## 4. Report

Measured distances and deceleration against the model of WP-S-04; the first measurement is also the first on-ground test under WP-V-07.
