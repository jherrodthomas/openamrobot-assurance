## 1. Purpose

Validation of personnel detection and the protective fields: test pieces per IEC 61496-3 and ISO 13855 at the field boundary, at speed, at every zone's field set; blind-zone tests below the plane and at the rear. Protective field dimensions are those of WP-S-04 at the measured stopping distance of WP-V-02.

## 2. Test specification

PD-nn: test piece, approach direction and speed, expected stop point, per field set and speed.

## 3. Blind zones

What is not detected, and the operating-environment limit or bumper that covers it.

## 4. Report

Results; the hold point for operation with people in the area (WP-M-01 §7).
