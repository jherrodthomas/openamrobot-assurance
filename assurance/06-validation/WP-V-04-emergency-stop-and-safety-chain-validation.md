## 1. Purpose

Validation of the emergency stop function and the whole safety chain on the robot: every E-stop device, latching and reset, contactor drop-out, brake engagement, behaviour on power loss and on restart (ISO 13850, EN 60204-1 §9.2, §18).

## 2. Test specification

ES-nn per device and per chain state.

## 3. Report

Response times, results, deviations.
