## 1. Purpose

Evaluation of unknown hazardous scenarios: structured field trials in the operating environment, with the robot in its validated configuration, looking for triggering conditions WP-C-10 did not list; acceptance criteria and what a finding does to the analyses (ISO 21448 §11 as the informative method).

## 2. Trial plan

Hours, zones, tasks, observers, what is logged.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

Per hazard class; what stops the trials.

## 4. Findings

Each becomes a triggering condition in WP-C-10 or a misuse in WP-C-06, through problem resolution.
