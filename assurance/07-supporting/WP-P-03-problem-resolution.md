## 1. Purpose

Problem resolution: how problems, anomalies and findings are raised, triaged, classified, analysed, fixed and closed; the issue templates and labels; the safety anomaly rule of WP-M-02 §9.

## 2. Intake and triage

GitHub issue templates (problem report, safety concern); labels `problem`, `safety-anomaly`, `confirmation-finding`, `qa-finding`, `risk`; triage within one working day for safety anomalies.

## 3. Classification

Severity classes A–D by worst credible outcome; root cause categories.

## 4. Analysis and closure

Root cause, corrective and preventive action, verification of effectiveness, closure by someone other than the analyst for class A and B.
