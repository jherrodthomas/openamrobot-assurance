## 1. Purpose

System FMEA of the robot at the level of WP-S-03's blocks and the functional layers: failure modes, effects on each safety function, detection, and the measures; the system-level input to the fault trees and to WP-H-03.

## 2. Method

IEC 60812; one table per block; severity by the hazard reached.

## 3. Tables

SFM-nn per block.

## 4. Findings

Single points of failure, undetected failures, items for the fault lists.
