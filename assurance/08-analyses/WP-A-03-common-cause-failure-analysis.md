Common cause failure analysis of the two-channel parts of the chain (ISO 13849-1 Annex F scoring): separation, diversity, design and application, assessment and analysis, competence, environment; the score per safety function and the measures that earn it.

## 2. Scoring per safety function

The Annex F table with the measures claimed and their evidence.

## 3. Shared resources

Power supply, wiring routes, enclosure, temperature, the operator.
