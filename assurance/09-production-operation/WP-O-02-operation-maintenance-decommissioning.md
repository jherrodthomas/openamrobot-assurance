## 1. Purpose

Operation, maintenance and decommissioning: daily checks, modes and their rules, maintenance and proof-test intervals of the chain, battery care, firmware updates, secure operation guidelines, decommissioning.

## 2. Operation

Pre-use checks, modes, what the operator may do, what to do on an anomaly.

## 3. Maintenance

Intervals, proof tests, replacement of wearing safety components, records.

## 4. Secure operation

Network, accounts, updates (62443-4-1 SG).

## 5. Decommissioning

Battery, data, keys.
