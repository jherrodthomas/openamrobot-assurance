## 1. Purpose

Information for use, markings and warnings (ISO 12100 §6.4, Machinery Regulation Annex III §1.7): the residual risks of WP-C-05 as warnings, the operating-environment limits of WP-C-02, markings on the robot, and the rule that a limitation that is only documented is not a control for a hazard that can kill.

## 2. Residual risks and warnings

From WP-C-05; each with its marking or instruction.

## 3. Operating limits

Zones, speeds, payload, battery, people; what is outside the case.

## 4. Markings

Plate, warning labels, E-stop and reset identification.
