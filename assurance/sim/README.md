## The tour

![The tour: a looping view of the whole warehouse plan. The 2.0 mobile manipulator reverses out of its dock, crawls out of the dock lane with field set FS-2c, takes the cross-aisle and aisle 2 with FS-1c, stops for a person who crosses ahead, waits, warns for 2 s and resumes, picks a tote at the station, carries it round aisle 1 with FS-4 past a bystander, places it on a drop shelf and docks again with the field muted in the last metre; a panel names the zone, speed, field set, pose, state and speed-up factor, situation chips light as they are reached, and a timeline shows the zones, the field length and a cursor](figures/fig-sim-tour-anim.svg)

*The tour.* CFG-002 on a closed route through the plan of WP-C-02 Figure 2-2. The speed of each step is the limit of the zone the robot is in (WP-C-02 §2) and the field set follows from the speed and the pose (the CFG-002 braked rows of WP-S-04 §4.1, FS-4 in the carry pose), so the set switches where the zone boundaries are. The crossing of IS-01 and the resume of IS-10 play at real time; the transit runs up to 16 times faster, and the factor is printed. The route and the drop shelf are the generator's, like the site. It plays in a browser; a viewer that shows the SVG as a still shows the robot docked. Synthetic: pictures only, no evidence credit.

Not built yet: the review dashboard of [`ideas-2d-views.md`](ideas-2d-views.md) §3 (e), deferred to the physics layer; a reaching or crouching pose for the person (blender README OI-1); the stopping-distance and field model of WP-S-04 run as a plant with the fault list of WP-V-05; a closed loop with the full navigation stack (planner, controller, costmaps and velocity smoother; the Gazebo scenarios drive the collision monitor directly). Each is a planned layer of the same pattern and is added here when it exists; none is a work product of [WP-M-00](../01-management/WP-M-00-work-product-register.md) until the process model gets an activity for it.
