# Welcome to the OpenAMRobot assurance wiki

Orientation for newcomers, contributors and reviewers. This wiki explains and links. It never states a safety
claim: those live in the [assurance record](../assurance/README.md), where they are reviewed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **OpenAMRobot is research hardware and this record is a draft prepared for engineering review.** Nothing has
> passed verification review, confirmation review or a functional safety assessment. The robot is not CE-marked,
> no safety function reaches any Performance Level today, and no configuration is approved for operation around
> people.

## What this is

The engineering assurance record for a safety-assured fork of [OpenAMRobot](https://github.com/openAMRobot), an
open mobile manipulator platform by Botshare LTD. The fork keeps upstream's code and adds the record around it:
machinery risk assessment, a hardwired safety chain with a Performance Level target, cybersecurity, machine-learning
safety, and a safety case published as it is built with the evidence for each claim and the known counter-evidence
against it, under the standards that apply to a machine. The [README](../README.md) is the long version.

## What this wiki is not

- **Not a work product.** Nothing here carries a document-control header or counts as evidence.
- **Not the place for hazard, requirement or safety-function text.** Each page links to the record that owns it.
- **Not illustrated by hand.** Every image is a figure that already exists in the repository or is rendered from
  its records at sync time.
- **Not edited in place.** Pages are kept in the repository under [`wiki/`](../wiki/README.md), reviewed by pull
  request, and mirrored here on merge. An edit made on the wiki itself is overwritten by the next sync.
